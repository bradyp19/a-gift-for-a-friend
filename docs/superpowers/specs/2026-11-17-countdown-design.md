# A gift for a friend

A small personal countdown for Angie. White background, black typography, colorful pixel characters. Plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript keeps this dependency-free; React would add unnecessary tooling for one screen.

First visit: centered uppercase pixel LOADING with black progress bar; then exact lowercase copy in large left-aligned Helvetica Neue/Arial with blinking caret: “hey angie. you got this. keep grinding. you're almost there until thanksgiving. this is a timer until you see me again.” Type over 8–10 seconds, hold five seconds, fade into timer. Remember completed intro locally; no replay control.

Countdown target: November 18, 2026 at 8 p.m. America/New_York, equivalent to 2026-11-19T01:00:00Z. Show days, hours, minutes, seconds. At zero show “finally. come here ♡” and celebrating corgi.
